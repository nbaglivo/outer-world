One sip at a time

A daily paper built at 06:30 from the newsletters in subs@nbaglivo.dev and, later, posts from the accounts the blog follows through warmhole. It lives in the nmail repository and shares its mail code.

Design · 16 October 2026 · spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-one-sip-at-a-time-design.md

built in milestones 1–4 fediverse, milestone 5 (warmhole reader API) shared with nmail

Architecture

Everything runs on the Mac. sip only reads: IMAP is opened read-only and fetched with peek, so building an edition never marks mail as seen. warmhole is the one server, and only the fediverse half needs it.

Components in the repository

One Go module, two entrypoints. nmail's packages stay where they are; the shaded band is what both apps use. sip's own packages live under internal/sip/. Arrows point from a package to what it imports.

Flows

Three things happen: the edition gets built, the 06:30 cutoff decides which edition you see, and (later) posts reach warmhole and then sip.
